REQUIRED NOTICE ELEMENTS (per 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3)):
To be effective, a DMCA takedown notice must include:
1. Physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or authorized agent
2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to be infringed
3. Identification of the infringing material and information reasonably sufficient to locate it (e.g., preset name, URL, user ID)
4. Contact information for the complaining party (name, address, phone, email)
5. Statement of good-faith belief that use is not authorized
6. Statement under penalty of perjury that information is accurate and the complaining party is authorized to act

COUNTER-NOTIFICATION PROCESS:
If you believe material was removed in error, you may submit a counter-notification including:
1. Your physical or electronic signature
2. Identification of the material removed and its location before removal
3. Statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed by mistake or misidentification
4. Your name, address, phone, email, and consent to jurisdiction of federal court in Travis County, Texas
5. Consent to accept service of process from the original complainant

REPEAT INFRINGER POLICY:
- First valid takedown: Content removed, user warned
- Second valid takedown: Content removed, sharing privileges suspended 30 days
- Third valid takedown: Account terminated, sharing privileges permanently revoked

FALSE CLAIMS:
Knowingly materially misrepresenting infringement under § 512(f) subjects the claimant to liability for damages, costs, and attorneys' fees.
